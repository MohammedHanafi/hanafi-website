### The state of education in Ghana

Ghana has the potential to be a world leader in education. However, the country has been struggling to make the most out of this sector. As much as Ghana is one of the top African countries with the highest literacy rate, there are still concerns about the quality of education. Ghana's educational system has made some progress over the years, but still has a long way to go. The country has been making efforts to improve the quality of education and make it more accessible for all Ghanaian citizens. The Ghanaian government has also been working to provide scholarships for students, increase funding for education, and make the sector more sustainable. The government has been trying to improve the overall education system by dealing with issues such as improving teacher quality, engaging the private sector, and strengthening the curriculum.

### Why is there a need for Inspiration Economy in our Educational System?

Education is a fundamental pillar of human development, particularly as it empowers women and young people to be self-reliant and to contribute to the prosperity of their communities. At the same time, it is one of the biggest sectors for investment, development, and entrepreneurship.

Despite the fact that education has become a fundamental part of every society, there are still many areas in which it can be improved—not just in developing countries, but in developed ones as well.

The education sector is currently facing many challenges that require solutions in order to improve the quality of life for all people.

#### Why are we seeing a decline in the educational system?

There are a few reasons why we are seeing a decline in the educational system. At the top of the list is the fact that many people don't believe that they can change their financial situations by pursuing education. A lack of financial support is one of the biggest reasons why people don't pursue an education, and can cause many people to drop out before they even finish. A lack of support from parents and teachers is another big reason why people don't go to school. If someone doesn't have a parent that can take them to and from school, or pay for their education, it is going to be very difficult for them to succeed.

### 3 ways to inspire current and upcoming generations

- **Develop a culture of curiosity.** Curiosity is the foundation of all human progress. Aspiring minds need to be exposed to different fields of study, even at an early age.
- **Focus on values.** Values are the building blocks for a positive culture. By focusing on values, such as honesty, appreciation, and kindness, students will learn important skills for life.
- **Equip young people with skills and tools.** In order to make a difference in the world, young people need to be equipped with the tools and skills that will allow them to thrive.

### Conclusion

The Inspiration Economy is a global movement focused on finding solutions to the world's biggest challenges. It is not just about creating jobs and new businesses, but also about identifying solutions to problems in order to improve the quality of life for all. Despite the fact that we have witnessed rapid change in technology and innovation over the last 10 years, education has continued to be a major challenge even at the tertiary level. However, there is hope for change. In order for the educational system to inspire positive change, students, parents, teachers, and the government must make an effort to improve the current state of education.
